### Scientific use-cases that can be addressed with a CTAO DL3 data set (event list + IRFs)

| Science goal / question                                         | What we would try to discover, measure or compare                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Locate and identify the sites of particle acceleration          | – Which Galactic objects (SNRs, PWNe, binaries, star-forming regions, etc.) emit VHE γ-rays? <br>– What is the spatial distribution of these emitters along the plane and in the nearby extragalactic sky?                                                                                                |
| Determine the acceleration mechanisms                           | – Are the observed spectra best described by leptonic (inverse-Compton) or hadronic (π⁰-decay) processes? <br>– How do spectral cut-offs and curvature vary with source class and environment?                                                                                                            |
| Map the propagation and impact of cosmic rays                   | – How does the γ-ray surface brightness change with distance from known accelerators (energy-dependent morphology)? <br>– What is the contribution of unresolved sources to the diffuse Galactic emission?                                                                                                |
| Search for PeVatrons                                            | – Which sources show γ-ray emission extending beyond 100 TeV, indicating acceleration of protons/nuclei to PeV energies?                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Characterise the population of VHE emitters                     | – What are the luminosity functions and duty cycles of SNRs, PWNe, binaries, and AGN? <br>– How does the population evolve with distance, environment or age?                                                                                                                                             |
| Perform a Galaxy-wide survey (GPS, Extragalactic, LMC)          | – Produce uniform sky maps and a catalog of point-like and extended sources down to a few mCrab sensitivity. <br>– Compare source counts and spatial distribution with predictions from population synthesis models.                                                                                      |
| Study the Galactic Centre region                                | – Resolve the morphology of the central source (point-like vs. extended ring). <br>– Measure spectra of Sgr A* and neighbouring molecular clouds to test cosmic-ray diffusion models.                                                                                                                     |
| Probe extreme environments around compact objects               | – What are the rapid (sub-minute to hour) flux variations of γ-ray binaries, micro-quasars and magnetars? <br>– How do flares from AGN, GRBs or Sgr A* evolve spectrally on short timescales?                                                                                                             |
| Multi-messenger counterpart searches                            | – Do γ-ray transients coincide with neutrino alerts (IceCube/KM3NeT) or gravitational-wave triggers? <br>– What γ-ray signatures accompany fast radio bursts or high-energy neutrino events?                                                                                                              |
| Measure the extragalactic background light (EBL)                | – Using spectra of distant blazars, can we recover the optical-IR photon field density and its evolution up to z ≈ 1?                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Search for intergalactic magnetic fields (IGMF)                 | – Is there evidence for extended pair halos or delayed pair echoes around distant TeV blazars, constraining B ≈ 10⁻¹⁶–10⁻¹² G?                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Test fundamental physics                                        | – Do photon arrival times show energy-dependent delays indicative of quantum-gravity-induced Lorentz-invariance violation? <br>– Is there a spectral modulation consistent with photon ↔ axion-like particle conversion in cosmic magnetic fields?                                                        |
| Indirect dark-matter searches                                   | – Are there excess γ-ray spectra or spatial distributions compatible with WIMP annihilation or decay in the Galactic halo, dwarf spheroidal galaxies, the LMC or galaxy clusters? <br>– Can we set limits on the velocity-averaged cross-section (∼3 × 10⁻²⁶ cm³ s⁻¹) over the mass range 200 GeV–20 TeV? |
| Investigate the high-energy behaviour of active galactic nuclei | – What are the time-resolved spectra of flaring blazars and radio galaxies? <br>– How does the γ-ray emission connect to jet physics, black-hole mass, and external photon fields?                                                                                                                        |
| Explore the ultra-high-energy cosmic-ray composition            | – Using the direct Cherenkov signal, can we separate heavy nuclei from protons and measure the spectrum up to the PeV region?                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Develop legacy data products for the community                  | – Provide calibrated sky maps, source catalogs, exposure and sensitivity files that can be cross-matched with radio, infrared, X-ray and neutrino surveys.                                                                                                                                                |

These goals illustrate the breadth of astrophysical and fundamental-physics investigations that become possible once a CTAO event list and its associated instrument response functions are available. Each question can be pursued by extracting spectra, morphologies, light curves, or sky maps from the data and comparing them with theoretical models or with observations at other wavelengths or messengers.

## Prompt 2: break each use case down into the Gammapy analysis it requires

[prompts/derive_use_cases/prompt_2.md](../prompts/derive_use_cases/prompt_2.md)

### How to do each of the listed VHE-γ-ray science cases with Gammapy

| Science goal                                                                            | Gammapy pieces you would use                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Typical workflow (high-level or low-level)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Locate & identify particle-acceleration sites (Galactic / extragalactic source census)  | Data ingestion: `gammapy.data.DataStore` (DL3) or `gammapy.maps.Map`/`IRFMap` (DL4). <br>Source catalog: `gammapy.catalog.SourceCatalogHGPS`, `SourceCatalog3FGL`, `SourceCatalogGammaCat` ... to get known objects and seed models. <br>Survey-mode reduction: `MapDatasetMaker` + `FoVBackgroundMaker`/`RingBackgroundMaker` + `SafeMaskMaker`. <br>Source detection: `TSMapEstimator` (or `ExcessMapEstimator`) → `find_peaks` (or external scikit-image). <br>Modelling: `SkyModel` with `PointSpatialModel` / `GaussianSpatialModel` and a simple `PowerLawSpectralModel`. <br>Fit: `Fit` (Cash statistic). <br>Population: `gammapy.astro.population` tools to simulate/compare with synthetic populations. | 1. Load all observations with `DataStore`. <br>2. Define a common geometry (e.g. WCS map covering the Galactic plane). <br>3. Run the makers to produce a `MapDataset`. <br>4. Run `TSMapEstimator` → locate excesses. <br>5. Build a `SkyModel` for each excess, fit jointly (`Fit`). <br>6. Export a source list (`SourceCatalogObject`) or feed into `gammapy.astro.population` for statistical studies. |
| Determine acceleration mechanisms (leptonic vs hadronic, spectral curvature, cut-offs)  | Spectral models: `PowerLawSpectralModel`, `LogParabolaSpectralModel`, `NaimaSpectralModel` (wraps Naima radiative models). <br>EBL absorption: `EBLAbsorptionNormSpectralModel`. <br>Fit: `Fit` (iminuit, SciPy, Sherpa back-ends). <br>Flux-point extraction: `FluxPointsEstimator`. <br>Joint multi-instrument likelihood: `Datasets` container (`MapDataset`, `SpectrumDataset`, `FluxPointsDataset`) → simultaneous fit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | 1. Reduce each instrument to a `SpectrumDataset` (or `MapDataset`). <br>2. Assemble them in a `Datasets` list. <br>3. Define a physical model (e.g. Naima IC + π⁰-decay) and attach to each dataset via `SkyModel`. <br>4. Run `Fit` → obtain parameters and errors. <br>5. Use `FluxPointsEstimator` to produce SED points for visual checks.                                                              |
| Map cosmic-ray propagation & impact (energy-dependent morphology, diffuse contribution) | 3-D analysis: `MapDataset` (binned counts + IRFs) with spatially extended models (`GaussianSpatialModel`, `ShellSpatialModel`). <br>Morphology estimators: `TSMapEstimator`, `ExcessMapEstimator`, `FluxMaps` from `gammapy.estimators`. <br>Population / diffusion: `gammapy.astro` (source-evolution models, diffusion calculators).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | 1. Build a 3-D geometry (energy axis + sky). <br>2. Reduce data with `MapDatasetMaker`. <br>3. Fit a spatial model that can vary with energy (energy-dependent `SpatialModel` or custom model). <br>4. Use `TSMapEstimator` to produce significance/morphology maps in separate energy bands. <br>5. Compare the radial profiles with diffusion models from `gammapy.astro`.                                |
| Search for PeVatrons (γ-rays > 100 TeV)                                                 | High-energy data: DL4/5 maps from CTA, HAWC, etc. <br>Spectral model: simple `PowerLawSpectralModel` (no cut-off) or `ExponentialCutoffPowerLawSpectralModel`. <br>Fit: `Fit` with Cash statistic on `MapDataset` or `SpectrumDatasetOnOff`. <br>Significance: `TSMapEstimator` in > 50 TeV bins; `WStatCountsStatistic` for ON/OFF.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | 1. Reduce the highest-energy events into a `MapDataset` (energy bins covering 30-300 TeV). <br>2. Compute TS maps per high-energy band (`TSMapEstimator`). <br>3. Fit a hard power-law to the spectrum of each candidate (`Fit`). <br>4. Test for a cutoff by adding `ExponentialCutoffPowerLawSpectralModel`.                                                                                              |
| Characterise VHE source population (luminosity function, duty cycle, evolution)         | Catalog access: `gammapy.catalog` to import existing γ-ray catalogs. <br>Population synthesis: `gammapy.astro.population` (PWNe, SNRs, pulsars, binaries). <br>Statistical tools: `gammapy.stats` for likelihood ratio tests, AIC/BIC (via scikit-learn).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | 1. Build a homogeneous catalog from `SourceCatalog*` objects. <br>2. Compute luminosities using distances (external table) and fitted fluxes. <br>3. Compare observed distributions with synthetic ones generated by `gammapy.astro.population`.                                                                                                                                                            |
| Galaxy-wide surveys (GPS, extragalactic, LMC)                                           | High-level interface: `gammapy.analysis.Analysis` driven by a YAML file (observation selection, reduction, excess-map, model fit, flux-point & light-curve production). <br>Makers: `MapDatasetMaker`, `FoVBackgroundMaker`, `SafeMaskMaker`. <br>Estimators: `FluxPointsEstimator`, `TSMapEstimator`, `LightCurveEstimator`. <br>Output: sky-maps, source catalog, exposure & sensitivity maps.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | 1. Write a YAML config describing the survey grid, exposure, and analysis steps. <br>2. Run `gammapy.analysis.Analysis` (or the CLI `gammapy analysis run <config.yaml>`). <br>3. The workflow automatically loops over all pointings, reduces data, produces TS/flux maps and a source catalog.                                                                                                            |
| Study the Galactic Centre region                                                        | Deep 3-D dataset: `MapDataset` with fine spatial binning (arc-minute). <br>Background: `FoVBackgroundMaker` with custom scaling, or `RingBackgroundMaker`. <br>Complex source modelling: multiple `SkyModel`s (point source for Sgr A*, Gaussian ring, diffuse template). <br>Fit: `Fit` on the joint dataset. <br>Diffuse component: use `gammapy.astro.darkmatter.JFactor` or template maps from molecular-cloud data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | 1. Reduce the GC observations with `MapDatasetMaker`. <br>2. Apply `FoVBackgroundMaker` + `SafeMaskMaker`. <br>3. Define a model set (Sgr A* point + ring + clouds). <br>4. Perform a joint likelihood fit (`Fit`). <br>5. Produce residual TS maps and spectra for each component.                                                                                                                         |
| Probe extreme environments around compact objects (sub-minute to hour variability)      | Temporal analysis: `LightCurveEstimator` (or `LightCurve` class) on 1-D or 3-D datasets. <br>Time binning: split observations with `Observation.select_time`. <br>Spectral model: fix shape, fit only normalisation per time bin (use `ConstantTemporalModel` or custom). <br>Fit backend: iminuit (fast) or alternative optimizer.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | 1. Load the flare observation(s) with `DataStore`. <br>2. Split into 10 min (or desired) slices (`Observation.select_time`). <br>3. For each slice fit a `PowerLawSpectralModel` (or keep fixed) → obtain flux. <br>4. Run `LightCurveEstimator` to generate the curve, plot with `gammapy.visualization`.                                                                                                  |
| Multi-messenger counterpart searches (neutrinos, GW, FRB, etc.)                         | Event filtering: `Observation.select_time`, `Observation.select_region`. <br>Catalog cross-match: `gammapy.catalog` + Astropy's `SkyCoord`. <br>Likelihood with external constraints: add a temporal/spatial prior via a custom `TemporalModel` or `SpatialModel`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | 1. Load the γ-ray data set. <br>2. Cut to the time window of the external alert. <br>3. Generate a TS map and look for excesses at the alert position. <br>4. If needed, run a joint fit adding a source with a fixed location and a temporal model matching the counterpart.                                                                                                                               |
| Measure the Extragalactic Background Light (EBL)                                        | EBL absorption: `EBLAbsorptionNormSpectralModel` (built-in Franceschini, Domínguez, etc.). <br>Spectral fitting: attach the absorption model to the intrinsic `PowerLawSpectralModel`. <br>Joint fit: use `Datasets` with several blazars (different redshifts).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | 1. Reduce each blazar observation to a `SpectrumDataset`. <br>2. Build a model intrinsic * ebl (`PowerLawSpectralModel` × `EBLAbsorptionNormSpectralModel`). <br>3. Fit all datasets simultaneously (`Fit`) to constrain the EBL scaling.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Search for intergalactic magnetic fields (IGMF) (pair halos / echoes)                   | Extended model: `GaussianSpatialModel` or custom halo template. <br>Temporal model: `TemporalModel` for delayed emission. <br>Fit: joint likelihood on `MapDataset` with halo component.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | 1. Produce high-energy and lower-energy maps (`MapDataset`). <br>2. Define a halo spatial model (size ∝ B-field). <br>3. Fit together with the point-source model; evaluate TS improvement to set limits on B.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Test fundamental physics (LIV, photon-ALP mixing)                                       | Custom spectral models: implement a `SpectralModel` with energy-dependent speed-of-light term or ALP-induced oscillations (wrap external code). <br>Fit: `Fit` with likelihood ratio test (`stat_sum`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | 1. Reduce data to a `SpectrumDataset`. <br>2. Define the standard source model plus a LIV/ALP term (custom `SpectralModel`). <br>3. Fit both hypotheses, compute ΔTS → significance.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Indirect dark-matter searches                                                           | DM J-factor: `gammapy.astro.darkmatter.JFactor` class. <br>DM spectral model: `gammapy.modeling.models.NormSpectralModel` with built-in annihilation spectra (or custom via `NaimaSpectralModel`). <br>Spatial template: DM density map (e.g. NFW) → `TemplateSpatialModel`. <br>Datasets: `MapDatasetOnOff` or `SpectrumDatasetOnOff` for dwarf/halo observations. <br>Fit: `Fit` → upper limits (`FluxPointsEstimator` with `n_sigma_ul`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | 1. Load the target observations (e.g. dwarf spheroidal). <br>2. Compute/attach the J-factor map. <br>3. Build a DM `SkyModel` (template × spectral). <br>4. Perform a likelihood fit (`Fit`). <br>5. Derive limits on ⟨σv⟩ using the `FluxPointsEstimator` or profile scans.                                                                                                                                |
| High-energy behaviour of AGN (time-resolved spectra, jet physics)                       | Light curves: `LightCurveEstimator`. <br>Time-dependent spectral fitting: `SkyModel` with `ConstantTemporalModel` per bin or a full `TemporalModel`. <br>Joint multi-instrument fit: `Datasets` with Fermi-LAT + CTA.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | 1. Reduce each flare into a `SpectrumDataset` per time slice. <br>2. Fit a spectral model per slice (or joint fit with temporal component). <br>3. Assemble SED evolution, compare with jet models (e.g. via Naima or Agnpy wrapped with `NaimaSpectralModel`).                                                                                                                                             |
| Ultra-high-energy cosmic-ray composition (direct Cherenkov)                             | Event-level analysis: read DL3 `EventList` with `DataStore`. <br>Feature extraction: add custom columns (e.g. `CHERENKOV_SIGNAL`) and feed to a classifier (scikit-learn). <br>Background separation: use `WStatCountsStatistic` for ON/OFF regions if needed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | 1. Load the raw events. <br>2. Compute Cherenkov light parameters (external code). <br>3. Train a supervised classifier on simulated proton/heavy-nuclei events. <br>4. Apply to data → composition spectra.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Legacy data products for the community                                                  | High-level pipeline: `gammapy.analysis` (YAML-driven) to produce calibrated `MapDataset`s, exposure maps, sensitivity maps, and source catalogs. <br>Export: `MapDataset.write`, `FluxPoints.write`, `SourceCatalog.write` (GADF-compatible). <br>Visualization: `gammapy.visualization` helper functions for publication-quality plots.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | 1. Write a YAML file describing the whole reduction (observation selection, geometry, makers, background, models). <br>2. Run the analysis (`gammapy analysis run config.yaml`). <br>3. The script writes out all DL5/6 products (maps, catalogs, SED tables) ready for cross-matching with other surveys.                                                                                                  |

#### Key Gammapy concepts used across all cases

- Data level handling – `DataStore` → DL3 events & IRFs; `MapDatasetMaker` / `SpectrumDatasetMaker` → DL4 binned products.
- Background estimation – `FoVBackgroundMaker`, `ReflectedRegionsBackgroundMaker`, `RingBackgroundMaker`.
- Modeling – `SkyModel` (spectral + spatial + temporal components); built-in models (`PowerLawSpectralModel`, `LogParabolaSpectralModel`, `GaussianSpatialModel`, `TemplateSpatialModel`), external physics models via `NaimaSpectralModel`, `EBLAbsorptionNormSpectralModel`.
- Fitting – `Fit` (iminuit default, also SciPy or Sherpa back-ends).
- Estimators – `FluxPointsEstimator`, `TSMapEstimator`, `LightCurveEstimator`, `WStatCountsStatistic`.
- High-level workflow – `gammapy.analysis.Analysis` (YAML configuration) or the CLI (`gammapy analysis`).
- Catalog & population tools – `gammapy.catalog`, `gammapy.astro.population`, `gammapy.astro.darkmatter`.
- Visualization – `gammapy.visualization` helpers for maps, spectra, light curves.

With these building blocks a typical VHE γ-ray analysis - from raw event lists to scientific results such as source catalogs, spectra, light curves, or physical constraints - can be scripted in a few lines of Python or executed automatically via a YAML configuration.
